Privacy Policy for PadCommand

Effective Date: September 12, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 15, 2026

PadCommand ("we," "our," or "us") provides a rental property and asset command center designed to help real estate owners, landlords, and households organize property records, track finances, manage receipts and leases, and schedule renewals.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When signing in via Google Sign-In or email, we collect your email address, name, and optional profile picture.
  • Property & Asset Data: Property addresses, purchase history, valuation, square footage, rent amounts, mortgage details, insurance policies, appliances, and paint color records.
  • Financial Records: Income and expense transactions, dates, categories, payment notes, and tags.
  • Documents & Photos: Uploaded lease agreements, receipts, invoices, and property photos selected from your camera, storage, or Google Drive.
  • Contacts / My Team: Names, phone numbers, company names, and email addresses of contractors, tenants, and service providers you manually add.
  • Reminders: Mortgage renewal, insurance policy, lease renewal, and property tax due date reminders.

B. Device Permissions

  • android.permission.CAMERA: Required to photograph receipts, document scans, and property assets directly in the app.
  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED: Required to allow selecting existing photos and documents from your device gallery.
  • android.permission.READ_CALENDAR / WRITE_CALENDAR: Used when you choose to export reminders to your device's calendar.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for secure cloud sync and authentication with our database.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used for standard notification alerts.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (legacy, older Android versions only): Used only on older Android versions to attach existing files.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise or approximate device location (GPS)
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Your device's phone contacts list (contacts you add are typed manually, not imported)
  • SMS, call logs, or phone state
  • Advertising identifiers for third-party ad networks

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and enhance PadCommand functionality:

  • Authenticate your account and sync data across your devices and household members.
  • Calculate portfolio financials, net cash flow, and tax summaries.
  • Generate downloadable CSV and text exports of your transaction records.
  • Deliver local reminders for crucial property deadlines.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information to third parties or advertisers. PadCommand contains no ads, ad networks, or third-party tracking/analytics SDKs.

3. Data Security & Storage

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the app and our servers is secured using modern TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Cloud Storage: Data is hosted with Supabase (PostgreSQL), protected with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies enabled on all household, property, financial, document, contact, and reminder tables, scoped strictly to authenticated household members.
  • Authentication Security: Sessions use encrypted secure device storage and OAuth 2.0 / PKCE sign-in flow.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or delete individual properties, transactions, documents, and photos at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud records by emailing moxyinv@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Records are permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Access & Correction: You may request a copy of your stored data or correct inaccurate information at any time by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

PadCommand is intended for adult property owners and managers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
