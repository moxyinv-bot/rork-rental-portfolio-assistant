WHAT PADCOMMAND ACTUALLY COLLECTS - PLAIN ENGLISH SUMMARY
(For your own understanding — not the legal Play Store policy)
Last checked against the app code: September 15, 2026

============================================================
SHORT ANSWER
============================================================
PadCommand collects only what you type in or upload yourself
(properties, money records, documents, photos, contacts, reminders,
and your login email/name). It does NOT collect your location,
does NOT record audio, does NOT read your phone's contact list,
does NOT track you for ads, and does NOT contain any advertising
or third-party analytics SDKs. Everything is stored in your own
private household space in the cloud, protected by database rules
that only let members of your household see it.

============================================================
WHAT THE APP DOES COLLECT (confirmed by reviewing the code)
============================================================

1. Account info
   - Email address, display name, and profile picture ONLY if you
     sign in with Google. If you sign in with email/password instead,
     only your email and name are stored.
   - Source: hooks/useAuth.tsx, lib/supabase.ts

2. Property records you enter
   - Address, purchase price, value, square footage, rent, mortgage
     and insurance details, property tax due dates, appliances, paint
     colors, notes. All of this is data YOU type in — the app does not
     pull this from anywhere external.
   - Source: hooks/portfolio-store.ts, types/property.ts

3. Financial transactions you enter
   - Income/expense amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, tags.
   - Source: hooks/portfolio-store.ts (transactions table)

4. Photos and documents YOU choose to upload
   - Receipts, lease PDFs, property photos — only ones you pick from
     your camera or your own device/Google Drive file picker. The app
     cannot see your other photos or files; it only receives the
     specific file you select in the picker dialog.
   - Source: expo-image-picker / expo-document-picker usage in
     add-transaction.tsx, create-lease-document.tsx, property/[id].tsx

5. Contacts you manually type into "My Team"
   - Name, phone, email, company, category, notes — typed in by you.
   - IMPORTANT: This does NOT read your phone's real contacts list.
     It is just a form you fill out inside the app.
   - Source: hooks/portfolio-store.ts (household_contacts table)

6. Reminders
   - Titles and due dates for mortgage/insurance/lease/tax renewals.
   - If you tap "add to calendar," the app writes ONE event to your
     device calendar app. It does not read your existing calendar
     events, only adds the ones you create.
   - Source: expo-calendar usage, lib/reminder-notifications.ts

============================================================
WHAT THE APP DOES NOT COLLECT
============================================================
- NO GPS / location tracking (no location permission requested)
- NO microphone / voice recording (no audio permission requested)
- NO reading of your phone's actual contacts list
- NO SMS, call log, or phone state access
- NO background data collection while the app is closed
- NO advertising ID collection
- NO third-party analytics or ad SDKs bundled in the app
- NO selling or sharing of your data with data brokers or advertisers

Confirmed by checking android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml —
only these permissions exist in the released app:
  CAMERA, INTERNET, READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR,
  READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, VIBRATE,
  plus two legacy storage permissions capped to old Android versions
  only (for compatibility with very old phones).

============================================================
HOW YOUR DATA IS SECURED
============================================================

1. Encrypted in transit
   - All requests between the app and the backend use HTTPS/TLS.
     Source: lib/supabase.ts (createClient over https:// URL)

2. Encrypted session storage on your device
   - Your login session is stored using the device's secure storage
     (Android Keystore-backed), not plain text.
     Source: expo-secure-store usage in lib/supabase.ts

3. Household-only data isolation (Row Level Security)
   - The database enforces, at the database level (not just app code),
     that a user can only read/write rows belonging to a household
     they are a verified member of. This applies to properties,
     transactions, receipts, reminders, lease documents, contacts,
     and reminder delivery logs.
     Source: supabase/migrations/20260901_production_rls_policies.sql
     and the follow-up RLS fix migrations applied in September 2026.

4. Private cloud file storage
   - Uploaded photos/receipts/leases are stored in access-controlled
     storage buckets, gated by the same household-membership check
     used for the database — a person outside your household cannot
     read your files even if they had a raw storage link.

5. No hardcoded secrets in the client
   - The publishable Supabase key baked into the app is the public
     anon key (by design, safe to ship in an app), NOT an admin key.
     Actual access is gated by the database security rules above,
     not by hiding the key.

============================================================
BOTTOM LINE
============================================================
If you never granted the app camera/calendar/photo access, it has
nothing beyond your account email/name. Everything else in the app
is information you deliberately typed in, photographed, or uploaded,
and it stays inside your private household's data — not shared,
not sold, not used for ads.
